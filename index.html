About Digital Jigawa

Digital Jigawa is a community-led initiative that equips young people across Jigawa state with practical digital skills, mentorship and pathways to jobs in tech. We partner with schools, local government and industry volunteers to deliver high-value training and community projects.

  • ✓ Free monthly workshops
  • ✓ Bootcamps & mentorship
  • ✓ Women-in-Tech programs
  • ✓ School outreach
